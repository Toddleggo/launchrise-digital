## 1. Amazon Book Description (paste into KDP)

**Nobody got fired. That's the part people miss.**

In 2026, economists studying millions of real payroll records found something quietly alarming: young workers in the jobs most exposed to AI were being hired far less than expected. No mass layoffs. No headlines. The doors that used to be open had simply stopped opening.

That's what change looks like before it's obvious.

Two years ago, Todd Lego knew bugger-all about AI. Something in his bones told him to pay attention, so he chose to learn instead of burying his head in the sand. He's not a scientist or a programmer. He's an ordinary bloke who spent two years learning, testing and making mistakes so you don't have to, and this is the plain-English book he wishes every family already had.

**Inside you'll find:**

- **The Task Test:** find out in about 20 minutes which parts of your job AI can do, which it can help with, and which still need you
- **The Five Moats:** Hands, Heart, Trust, Taste and Accountability, the human strengths AI struggles to take
- **If You Do X, Start Learning Y:** practical next steps for more than 25 common jobs, from receptionists to truck drivers
- **The Income Tripod:** a simple way to build a household that can survive a job loss, including a savings ladder for families living week to week
- **Earning with AI from $0, $100 and $1,000:** realistic paths, no get-rich-quick nonsense
- **The 30-Day AI Reskill Challenge:** one small task a day
- **The Family Safe Word:** the two-minute defence against voice-clone and deepfake scams
- **The Family AI Preparedness Plan:** a checklist you can actually finish
- **What your kids should learn now,** and why reading, writing and maths matter more than ever
- **Nine five-year roadmaps** for employees, tradies, parents, students, business owners, professionals, creators, entrepreneurs and anyone out of work
- **The AI Resilience Scorecard:** see where your family stands, out of 50

Every forward-looking claim is labelled **Happening now**, **Trajectory**, **Plausible scenario** or **Wild card**, so you always know what's fact and what's forecast. Every major claim is sourced in the Notes.

No hype. No doom. No jargon. Just what's really happening and what to do about it.

**Read it. Then give it to someone you love.**
