# Future-Proof Your Family — Fact-Check Report (FINAL)

**Book:** *Future-Proof Your Family: How to Protect Your Job, Your Income and the People You Love in the Age of AI* by Todd Lego
**Date checked:** 1 October 2026 (all items)
**Result:** 37 of 37 original flags resolved. **0 unresolved.** No ⚠️, TODO, CHECK or [AUTHOR STORY] markers remain in the manuscript, PDF or DOCX.

**Status key:** VERIFIED = confirmed against the cited source and kept · REVISED = wording or figure changed to match the best source · REMOVED = cut because it could not be verified reliably or was not needed.

**Method:** primary sources first (court records, statutes, government statistics, peer-reviewed papers, company filings and official statements). Where only a company or vendor source exists, the book says so. Exposure, task automation, augmentation, job transformation, displacement and new-job creation are kept as separate terms (defined in Chapter 6). Forward-looking statements carry the reader-facing labels Happening now / Trajectory / Plausible scenario / Wild card.
