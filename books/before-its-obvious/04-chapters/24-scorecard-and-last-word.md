# A Last Word

I want to finish where I started. With a promise, and a truth.

The truth is this: nobody knows exactly what the next five years hold. Not me. Not the people building these systems. Not the experts on television. Anyone who tells you precisely what 2031 looks like is guessing — and many of them are selling you something.

But you've now seen what *is* known. You've seen the payroll data, the doubling clock, the scam numbers, the research on kids and tutors, the robots and the driverless trucks, the three futures and their warning signs. You know more about what's actually happening than the vast majority of people around you.

More importantly, you've got a plan.

A safe word that could stop someone you love from losing their savings. A Task Test that shows you exactly where your job is strong and where it's exposed. A cushion that buys you time. A second income, even a small one, that means you're never standing on one leg. Skills that most people won't have for years. Kids who'll learn to think with AI instead of letting it think for them. A family that talks about this together instead of worrying about it alone.

None of that depends on knowing which future arrives. All of it makes you stronger in every one of them.

---

I've spent a lot of this book on what could go wrong. I've done that because you deserve honesty, and because the risks are real.

But I want to leave you with the other side, because it's just as real.

We might be entering a period where one person, with the right skills, can do what used to take a team. Where a kid in a village with a phone can get tutoring that used to be available only to the rich. Where a tradie gets their evenings back. Where a nurse spends more time with patients and less with paperwork. Where a mum with a good idea and no capital can build a business from her kitchen table. Where someone who lost their job at fifty finds, to their own surprise, that their twenty-five years of experience is worth *more* in a world of machines that have read everything but never lived anything.

Those things are already starting to happen. They'll happen more for people who are ready.

---

You picked up this book because some part of you sensed that something big was changing, and you didn't want to be caught standing still.

Trust that instinct. It's the same instinct that has kept people safe through every great change in history — the one that says *pay attention, get ready, look after your people.*

You can't control how fast AI moves.

You *can* control how ready you are when it arrives.

And by reading this far, you're already more ready than you were.

Now go and do your next 24 hours.

And then — if this book helped — give it to someone you love.
