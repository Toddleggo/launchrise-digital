# Chapter 23
## Your Plan

This is where it all comes together.

You've seen what's happening now. You've seen the three possible futures. You've tested your job, looked at your money, learned to use AI, protected your family and thought about your kids.

Now you need a plan. Not a vague intention. A plan with dates on it.

Below are nine roadmaps — one for each kind of reader this book was written for. Find yours. If you're more than one (most people are — a parent who's also an employee, a tradie who's also a small-business owner), combine them. Take the most urgent items from each.

Every roadmap has the same seven time horizons:

**NEXT 24 HOURS · NEXT 7 DAYS · NEXT 30 DAYS · NEXT 6 MONTHS · NEXT 12 MONTHS · NEXT 3 YEARS · NEXT 5 YEARS**

The early steps are small on purpose. Momentum matters more than ambition at the start. Do the next 24 hours today.

---

## 1. THE EMPLOYEE
*You work for someone else, mostly at a desk or in an office-based role.*

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- Do the Task Test (Chapter 6). Get your Automate %.
- Set up your family safe word.

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Start the 30-Day Challenge (Chapter 15).
- Use AI on one Assist task every day this week.
- Find out your employer's AI policy.

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Finish the 30-Day Challenge.
- Volunteer to trial one AI tool for your team.
- Start an automatic savings transfer.
- Reconnect with three people in your industry outside your company.

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Be known as "the AI person" on your team.
- Move at least 10% of your time from Automate tasks toward Assist and Anchor work.
- Keep a "wins file": specific results you've achieved, with numbers.
- Reach one month of expenses in your cushion.
- Choose your "next door" (Chapter 11), even if you don't plan to use it yet.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Redo the Task Test. Has your exposure changed?
- Ask for a role, project or title that reflects your new skills.
- Build a small second income (Chapter 13).
- Reach three months' cushion.

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Be in a role where you direct AI rather than compete with it.
- Have a professional network that would help you find work within weeks if needed.
- Have a second income leg that could grow if you needed it.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Be in work built around Anchor tasks — judgement, relationships, responsibility.
- Six months' cushion. Low high-interest debt.
- Able to change jobs from a position of choice, not panic.

## 2. THE TRADESPERSON
*You work with your hands — electrician, plumber, builder, mechanic, cleaner, landscaper, etc.*

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- Use AI to write one quote, invoice reminder or social post tonight.
- Set up your family safe word.

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Ask your last three happy customers for a review.
- Identify your top three most-hated admin tasks and try AI on each.

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Complete the 30-Day Challenge, focusing on business admin.
- Set up one automation (bookings, reminders or invoice follow-ups).
- Look up one specialisation course (electrical, solar/battery, EV charging, HVAC, data centre work).

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Get your admin time down by at least half.
- Use the freed time for paid work or family.
- Start or book the specialisation course.
- Build a proper Google Business profile and review base.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Complete your specialisation.
- Raise your rates to reflect it.
- Build a three-month cushion (self-employed income is lumpy).

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Be a recognised specialist in a growing area.
- Consider taking on an apprentice or employee — AI admin makes running a bigger business manageable.
- Consider training or mentoring others entering the trade.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Run a business, or hold a role, that's hard to replace and in high demand.
- Have income that doesn't depend entirely on your own hands (staff, training, a second stream) — protection against injury as much as against AI.

---

## 3. THE SMALL-BUSINESS OWNER
*You run a business with a handful of staff or fewer.*

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- List every task in your business that happens on a computer.
- Set up your family safe word — and a "verify payments by phone" rule for your business.

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Try AI on your three biggest admin time-sinks.
- Check your cybersecurity basics: two-step verification, backups, password manager.

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Pick one process (enquiries, bookings, quotes, marketing, bookkeeping) and redesign it with AI.
- Train your staff in the basics. Share Chapter 14 with them.
- Set a rule: any change of supplier bank details must be verified by phone on a known number.

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Redesign two more processes.
- Decide your approach to staff: how will you redeploy time saved? (Growth, better service, training — not just cuts.)
- Identify where the Human Premium applies in your business and make it visible.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Measure: hours saved, revenue per staff member, customer satisfaction.
- Explore a new product or service that AI makes possible.
- Build a business cash reserve.

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Run a business that does more with the same team, competing on quality and relationships.
- Have staff who are skilled at directing AI.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Be the business in your area known for being both efficient *and* human.

## 4. THE PARENT
*Whatever your job, you're raising kids through this.*

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- Set up the family safe word.
- Say to your child: *"If anyone ever threatens you with pictures — real or fake — tell me. You'll never be in trouble."*

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Ask your kids to show you how they use AI. Watch without judging.
- Check apps, age ratings and parental controls.
- Agree phones out of bedrooms at night.

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Complete Section 1 and 4 of the Family AI Preparedness Plan (Chapter 18).
- Try the "tutor prompt" (Chapter 14) with your child on real homework.
- Start one hands-on skill together.

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Agree family rules for AI and schoolwork with your kids (and their teachers).
- Make sure each child has one physical/creative activity and one team/social activity.
- Talk to your child's school about their AI policy.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Hold your first annual family AI conversation (Chapter 18).
- For teenagers: help them start a proof project.
- For teens nearing school-leaving: have the honest "university, trade or something else?" conversation (Chapter 19).

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Kids who use AI on the Smart side of the fork.
- Kids with durable skills: thinking, writing, people, hands, money, resilience.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Young people who can learn anything, adapt to anything, and know they can always come home to talk.

## 5. THE STUDENT
*You're at school, university or in training.*

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- Use AI as a tutor, not an answer machine, on one piece of study (the tutor prompt, Chapter 14).
- Set a safe word with your family.

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Do the 30-Day Challenge alongside your studies.
- Look up ten job ads for jobs you might want. What skills do they ask for?

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Start a proof project — something real you can show.
- Get a part-time job, volunteer role or internship if you don't have one.

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Build your proof project into something you'd show an employer.
- Meet five people working in fields you're interested in. Ask how they got there and what's changing.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Make a deliberate decision about your path: which moats will your career have?
- Be excellent at directing AI in your chosen field.
- Keep costs and debt as low as you reasonably can.

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Have real work experience, a portfolio of proof and a network.
- Be in your first proper role — or running something of your own.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Be established in work with strong moats, with the skill to keep learning whatever comes next.

---

## 6. THE PROFESSIONAL
*You're licensed or highly trained — health, law, education, engineering, finance, etc.*

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- Find out your organisation's AI policy.
- Write down three mistakes AI would likely make in your field that only you'd catch.

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Use an approved AI tool for one piece of documentation or admin.
- Read one serious piece on AI in your profession (your professional body likely has guidance).

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Complete the 30-Day Challenge.
- Offer to help your organisation trial or write policy for AI tools.

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Cut your admin time significantly using approved tools.
- Reinvest the time in the human core of your work.
- Become a go-to person on AI in your team or practice.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Explore one "AI bridge" opportunity: training colleagues, consulting, writing, speaking.
- Consider a fractional/advisory second income (Chapter 13).

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Be known as a professional who combines deep expertise with AI fluency.
- If your profession's junior pipeline is shrinking, help design how new professionals are trained.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Hold a role built on judgement, relationships and accountability — the parts of your profession that grow in value.

---

## 7. THE CREATOR
*You write, design, film, make music, build audiences or make things.*

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- Write down your taste: three things in your field that are brilliant, three that are rubbish, and why.

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Build one thing with AI you couldn't have built alone.
- Publish one piece of work under your own name.

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Choose your niche — the specific people you serve and the specific thing you know.
- Publish consistently (weekly).
- Use AI for research, production and admin. Bring the voice and the judgement yourself.

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Build a small, direct audience you own (an email list, not just followers).
- Position yourself as a director/editor/strategist, not just a producer.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Have at least two income streams from your creative work (e.g., client work plus a product, or a newsletter plus workshops).
- Make "human-made" or "lived experience" part of your offer where it's true.

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Be known for something specific.
- Have an audience that follows *you*, not just the platform.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Run a creative business where your taste, voice and trust are the product, and AI does the heavy lifting behind the scenes.

---

## 8. THE ENTREPRENEUR
*You're starting or want to start something.*

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- Write your one-sentence offer: *"I help [who] with [what] so they can [result]."*

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Talk to five potential customers about their problem. Listen more than you pitch.
- Do one piece of work for one of them.

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Get your first paying customer, however small.
- Use AI for your website, marketing, admin and research. Spend your own time on customers.

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Get to ten paying customers.
- Build the systems (with AI) that let you serve more without burning out.
- Keep costs low. Stay close to customers.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Know your numbers: revenue, costs, time per customer.
- Decide whether to grow, specialise or pivot.

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Run a business with more reach than its headcount suggests.
- Have built something around a real problem AI makes solvable — or a Human Premium AI makes valuable.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Own an asset — a business, a brand, a system — that earns without depending on every hour of your time.

## 9. THE PERSON CURRENTLY UNEMPLOYED
*You're between jobs, recently laid off, or struggling to get in.*

First: I'm sorry. It's hard, and it's harder when you're told the world is changing and you feel left behind. You're not too late. You're earlier than most people who'll face this.

**NEXT 24 HOURS**
- Apply for any support you're entitled to. Don't wait.
- Write down five things you're good at. Include things outside work.
- Set up your family safe word. (People in financial stress are prime scam targets.)

**NEXT 7 DAYS**
- Start the 30-Day Challenge. It's free, and it gives your days structure.
- Do the Task Test for your last job, and find your row on the Reskill Map (Chapter 11).
- Tell ten people you're looking. Be specific about what you want.

**NEXT 30 DAYS**
- Finish the 30-Day Challenge.
- Start a proof project for your chosen "next door."
- Do one piece of free or paid work for someone using your new skills.
- Use AI to tailor every application and practise every interview.

**NEXT 6 MONTHS**
- Land work — even if it's a stepping stone rather than the destination.
- Build a small side income if you can (Chapter 13 — the $0 paths).
- Keep learning daily.

**NEXT 12 MONTHS**
- Be in work, or running something of your own, with AI skills that most of your peers don't have.
- Start rebuilding your cushion, however slowly.

**NEXT 3 YEARS**
- Be in a role with stronger moats than the one you lost.
- Have a second income leg and a network that means you'll never be starting from zero again.

**NEXT 5 YEARS**
- Look back on this period as the turning point. Many people will.

### The single most important line in this chapter

Whatever your roadmap, the hardest step is the first one. Not because it's difficult — most of the first steps take minutes — but because it's so easy to put off.

So here's my ask: **do your NEXT 24 HOURS today.** Not tomorrow. Today.

Then do the next seven days. Then the next thirty.

Five years from now, you'll either be glad you started today, or wishing you had. There isn't really a third option.
