# PHASE 6–7 — STRUCTURAL & HUMAN-VOICE EDIT NOTES

**Done in draft 1:**
- Banned-phrase sweep (delve, landscape, leverage, unlock, harness, etc.): **0 hits**.
- Removed unverifiable author-experience claims ("I've watched…", "every parent I know…") that the author hasn't supplied.
- Every chapter ends with DO THIS NOW + Research Notes.
- Consistent frameworks across chapters (Forecast Labels, A-A-A, Five Moats, Tripod, Safe Word, Director's five jobs).

**Recommended for the human editor / author pass:**
1. **Insert real author stories** at every `[AUTHOR STORY]` marker. This is the single biggest step in making the book feel undeniably human.
2. **Reduce em-dashes** in body prose by ~30% (a known "machine-written" tell); swap for full stops or commas.
3. **Vary chapter openings** — several open with a direct address; mix in more scene-based openings once author stories are in.
4. **Read aloud test** on Ch. 1, 6, 16 (the "must-read" chapters).
5. **Localise** — decide US vs AU/UK spelling and examples for the primary market; currently mixed British spelling with mostly US data.
6. Consider trimming Ch. 7 and Ch. 11 role lists slightly for pace, or moving the full lists to an appendix.
