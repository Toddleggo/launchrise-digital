# TARGET-READER ANALYSIS

## The one reader we write to

**Call them "Sam."** 34–52. Works a real job. Has people depending on them. Uses ChatGPT maybe once a week, half as a toy. Has seen the headlines. Lies awake some nights doing sums.

Sam is not scared of technology. Sam is scared of **being the last to find out.**

What Sam says out loud: *"It's all hype, isn't it?"*
What Sam thinks at 2am: *"What if it isn't, and I'm the idiot who didn't see it coming?"*

Also: **the adult child worried about Mum and Dad.** People 60+ lost $7.75B to online crime in 2025 (FBI IC3). This reader buys the book *as a gift.* Big commercial lever.

## What they've already read (and why it didn't help)

- **Hype books / YouTube:** "Make $10k/month with AI." Feels like a scam. They're right.
- **Doom content:** "AI will take every job by 2027." Paralysing. No actions.
- **Tech books:** Written for people who already understand it.
- **Think-tank reports:** True, useless at the kitchen table.

**The gap:** nobody has written the calm, honest, *practical* book that sits between the hype and the doom and says: *"Here's what's real. Here's what's guesswork. Here's what you do on Monday."*

## Emotional journey we design for

1. **Recognition** — "This person gets exactly how I feel."
2. **Clarity** — "Oh. So *that's* what's actually happening."
3. **Honest alarm** — "This is real, and it's closer than I thought." (evidence, never theatre)
4. **Relief** — "But it's not hopeless, and I'm not too late."
5. **Competence** — "I just did something. I can do this."
6. **Protectiveness** — "I need to sort this for my kids / my parents."
7. **Momentum** — "I've got a plan for the next 5 years."
8. **Sharing** — "Everyone I love needs to read this."

## Buying triggers

- A news headline about layoffs or a deepfake scam.
- A child asking "should I still go to uni?"
- A parent nearly falling for a scam call.
- A boss mentioning "AI efficiency."
- Seeing a younger colleague do in 20 minutes what takes them a day.

## Reading reality (design rules)

- Many readers will read on a phone, in 10-minute bursts. → **Short chapters. Short paragraphs. A clear "Do this now" box at the end of every chapter.**
- Many will skip to "their" chapter. → Every chapter must stand alone.
- Many will never finish. → The **first 30 pages** must already change their life (safe word, Task Test, first AI habit).

## Language rules

- Year-10 reading level for mechanics. Adult-level for ideas.
- Every technical word explained in the same sentence it appears.
- Never "leverage," "landscape," "unlock," "delve," "in today's fast-paced world," "game-changer," "revolutionise."
- Use "you" and "we." Talk like a smart friend across a kitchen table.
