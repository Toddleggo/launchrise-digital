## 7. Launch & Marketing Plan

### Pre-launch (4–6 weeks before)
- **Lead magnet:** free printable *Family AI Preparedness Plan* + *AI Resilience Scorecard* (PDF) → email list.
- **Landing page** with the scorecard as an interactive quiz (high shareability).
- **ARC team:** give 50–100 advance copies to readers in exchange for honest reviews on launch day (follow Amazon review rules: no paid reviews, and disclose free copies).
- **Post 3×/week** using hooks #1–10; start the "30 Days of AI" series.
- **Pitch 20 podcasts** (parenting, personal finance, careers, small business, trades). Angle: "The scam every family needs to know about" and "The hiring freeze nobody announced."

### Launch week
- Ebook at $0.99–$2.99 to drive rank; paperback full price.
- Daily email to list; daily short videos.
- Ask ARC readers to post reviews on day one.
- Press release angle: *"New book shows families how to beat AI voice-clone scams in two minutes."*
- Offer the free Family Plan PDF inside the book with a link, which grows the list from every reader.

### Post-launch (months 1–6)
- **Amazon Ads:** start with auto campaigns plus exact-match keywords from KDP slots. Small daily budget, scale the winners.
- **Gift pushes:** Mother's Day / Father's Day ("protect your parents"), back-to-school ("prepare your kids"), graduation ("the book every grad needs").
- **Local media:** community radio, local papers. The scam-safety angle works well here.
- **Workshops:** run "AI for Families" or "Scam-Proof Your Parents" sessions at libraries and community centres. Sell books at the back of the room.
- **Corporate/bulk sales:** pitch HR teams on employee AI-readiness and pitch banks and credit unions on customer scam education.

### Spin-offs (high ROI 💰)
1. **30-Day AI Reskill Workbook** (Gumroad/KDP low-content), cheap to make from Ch. 15.
2. **What I'd Tell My Kids**, a parent-focused short book from Ch. 17 and 19.
3. **The Tradie's AI Playbook**, a niche short book from Ch. 9 and 13.
4. **Annual updated edition** ("2027 Edition"), easy to produce because the Forecast Labels make updating simple, and it gives readers a reason to buy again.
5. **Online course / cohort** version of the 30-Day Challenge.
