## PART I — IT HAS ALREADY STARTED

### Ch 1 — The Hiring Freeze Nobody Announced
- Opens: "Nobody got fired."
- The Stanford *Canaries* data: 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed jobs ~19% behind; through hiring, not firing.
- Balance: Yale Budget Lab — no economy-wide collapse. Unemployment ~4.1%.
- Why both are true at once. The bottom rung of the ladder is where change shows first.
- Recent grads unemployment above national rate — historically unusual.
- Challenger: AI top stated reason for announced cuts in 2026 — with caveat.
- Frame: change looks like *absence* before it looks like *events*.
- **Do this now:** write down 3 tasks you did this week; ask one AI to do one of them; notice how you feel.

### Ch 2 — What AI Actually Is (Without the Jargon)
- The world's most well-read intern who's never been outside.
- Chatbot → Assistant → Agent → Robot: the four steps, with a kitchen analogy (recipe book → cook who reads recipes → cook who shops, cooks and cleans → cook with hands).
- Multimodal: it can see, hear, speak.
- Why it's wrong with confidence ("hallucination" explained).
- What it's not: conscious, a search engine, a person.
- **Do this now:** have your first real conversation with an AI about your actual job.

### Ch 3 — The Doubling Clock
- METR's measurement explained with a stopwatch.
- Doubling every ~7 months over six years; faster since 2024.
- What doubling feels like: the lily-pad pond.
- Honest caveats: 50% ≠ reliable; software-heavy tasks; measurement limits.
- The Jagged Edge (Harvard/BCG): +40% quality inside the frontier, 19 points worse outside it.
- METR's own study where devs felt faster but were slower — the perception trap.
- Frame: *Trust the trend, distrust any single number.*
- **Do this now:** find one thing AI does brilliantly for you and one thing it gets wrong.
